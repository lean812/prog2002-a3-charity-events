Our mission

To connect caring people in Liuzhou with local causes through community events that raise funds and awareness for the people who need them most.

What we do

We host a year-round programme of fundraising events, each tied to a specific local cause such as rural school libraries, children's healthcare, scholarships and support for elderly residents.

How your ticket helps

Every ticket registration is a donation. Event pages show the fundraising goal and live progress, so you can see exactly how your support contributes.
